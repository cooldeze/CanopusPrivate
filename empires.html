Regulus — Empires
Hindu association: Magha Nakshatra
Regulus, "the little king," has been read as a royal star across nearly every ancient sky-watching tradition. In Hindu astronomy it anchors Magha — "the mighty one" — the nakshatra of thrones, ancestry, and inherited authority, ruled by the Pitrs, the spirits of one's forebears. Magha's symbol is a palanquin: power carried forward by those who came before. This section is named Regulus because every empire mapped here is exactly that — authority built on what preceded it, extending outward until it reshaped the map, and eventually passing, as all thrones do, to something else.
