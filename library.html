Library of Philosophy

Nineteen schools of thought, spanning three thousand years and every inhabited continent — each one a different answer to the same handful of questions: how should we live, what can we know, and what do we owe each other.

Library of Speeches

Moments where spoken words changed the course of events — courtrooms, battlefields, funerals, and podiums where a single address outlived the occasion that produced it. Full texts aren't reproduced here (many remain protected works); each entry covers who spoke, when, why it mattered, and what it left behind.
