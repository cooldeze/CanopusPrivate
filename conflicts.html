03 — Summary
The unfinished business of history

The punitive peace after WWI incubated WWII. WWII's aftermath produced the Cold War and decolonization. Decolonization produced both the partition of India — source of a Kashmir dispute that still triggers wars between two nuclear powers — and Israel's founding amid the Middle East's colonially-drawn borders. The Cold War's Afghan proxy war armed the movements behind 9/11; the response in Iraq handed Iran a regional foothold used for two decades, built through Hezbollah, Hamas, and allied militias — the machinery behind October 7. That war's aftermath left Iran weak enough that Israel and the US judged direct war winnable in 2025 and 2026. In January 2026, that same appetite for direct unilateral action reached the Western Hemisphere, when a two-hour US raid ended thirteen years of Maduro rule in Venezuela. Each conflict didn't just end a problem — it created the conditions for the next one.

Antares (Conflicts)
Jyeshtha Nakshatra
Antares is Scorpius's fiery red heart, and in Hindu astronomy it anchors Jyeshtha — "the eldest" — the nakshatra of seniority, protection, and hard-won authority, ruled by Indra, king of the gods and wielder of the thunderbolt against chaos. Conflicts is named Antares because every war mapped here is guarded ground fought over by rival claims to seniority and protection — each one, in some real sense, the unfinished business of the one before it.
